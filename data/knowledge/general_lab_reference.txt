TITLE: General Laboratory Reference & Diagnostic Values
SOURCE: Clinical Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI) Guide
TOPIC: Complete Blood Count (CBC), Metabolic Panel, Hemoglobin A1c

SUMMARY:
1. Complete Blood Count (CBC) evaluates red blood cells (RBC), white blood cells (WBC), and platelets. White blood cell elevation (leukocytosis) frequently indicates immune response, infection, or physiological stress.
2. Fasting Plasma Glucose >= 126 mg/dL or Hemoglobin A1c >= 6.5% are diagnostic criteria for Diabetes Mellitus. Values between 5.7% and 6.4% indicate Prediabetes.
3. Serum Creatinine and Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN) reflect renal filtration capacity. Mild elevations should be correlated with hydration status and current medications.
4. Patient communication guideline: Lab test reference ranges represent statistical averages of healthy populations. An out-of-range flag does not equate to a definitive disease diagnosis without clinical correlation.
