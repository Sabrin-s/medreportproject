TITLE: Gastroenterology and Hepatic Imaging Guidelines
SOURCE: American College of Gastroenterology Clinical Handbook
TOPIC: Endoscopy, Abdominal Ultrasound, Liver Enzymes (ALT/AST)

SUMMARY:
1. Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) are enzymes primarily concentrated in hepatocytes. Elevated levels reflect liver cell irritation or injury.
2. Hepatic steatosis (fatty liver) detected on abdominal ultrasound indicates lipid accumulation in liver parenchyma. Grade 1 is mild and often reversible through lifestyle interventions.
3. Upper endoscopy (EGD) visualizes the esophageal, gastric, and duodenal mucosa. Erythema or mild gastritis describes superficial inflammation of the stomach lining.
4. Patient communication guideline: Elevated liver enzymes describe laboratory values above reference ranges. They necessitate diagnostic context including alcohol intake, medication review, and metabolic status.
